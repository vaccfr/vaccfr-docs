# Demande fast-track auprès de VATEUD

Conformément à la politique fast-track de VATEUD, le vACC Training Director ou son Deputy doit envoyer un mail à VATEUD (atc-td@vateud.net).

Dès lors que le screenshot du profil IVAO / licence du candidat est déposée sur son profil core. Voici le mail à envoyer à VATEUD.

## Mail à envoyer

Dear VATEUD Training Department,

We would like to submit a fast-track request for the following student: **10000000 - John Doe**.

The candidate has received an introduction to the tools as well as to VATSIM procedures. In addition, the candidate was evaluated on Sweatbox on a **Tower / Approach** position under the same conditions as our students at the end of their **S2/S3** training.

As the performance was satisfactory, we respectfully submit for your consideration an **S2/S3** fast-track for this student.

We have uploaded the candidate's **ATC license / IVAO profile** to their VATEUD core profile.

We remain at your disposal should you require any further information.

Best regards,

## Issue

VATEUD peut demander des clarifications par mail. VATEUD peut aussi refuser le fast-track. Si tout est ok, VATEUD donne accès à l'élève un test théorique. Si réussite, la vACC peut faire la recommandation pour le grade suivant.
