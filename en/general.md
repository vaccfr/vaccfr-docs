# First steps

## What is Frenc vACC?
French vACC is the official french community of pilots and controlers on the VATSIM network. French vACC is responsible for **Air Trafic Services** and ATC trainnig in <u>metropolitan France</u>.

We are also here to provide some guidance to new pilots on the network.

## How are VATSIM entites structured?
The VATSIM network is structured in different entoty levels, more or less dependant on one another.

At the lowest level are **vACCs** or sub-divisions, then **divisions** like VATEUD (a group of vACCs), then come **regions** as VATEMEA (a group of divisions).

Above all is the **VATSIM : board of governors (BoG)** responsible for the entire network.

## Is there ATC planned in France?
The welcome page of <a href="https://vatsim.fr/" target="_blank">https://vatsim.fr/</a> displays all daily bookings made and currently online ATC. By connecting on your dashboard at <a href="https://vatsim.fr/app" target="_blank">https://vatsim.fr/app</a>, you can see bookings up to 15 days in advance.

## Where can I find French vACC events?
French vACC has a wide varity of events from fly-in to internationnal events.
You can find more details and all events here : <a href="https://vatsim.fr/events" target="_blank">https://vatsim.fr/events</a>

## What are pilot ratings and how to obtain them?
On VATSIM, pilot ratings are obtained in the following order :

- **P0** beginner pilot
- **P1** private pilot (**P**rivate **P**ilot **L**icense)
- **P2** **I**nstrument **R**ating
- **P3** **C**ommercial **P**ilot **L**icense / Multi-Engine Piston
- **P4** **A**irline **T**ransport **P**ilot **L**icense

If you are a pilot in real life, you can get an equilavent VATSIM pilot rating by submitting your licence at <a href="https://my.vatsim.net/pilots/train" target="_blank">https://my.vatsim.net/pilots/train</a>.

If you want get this ratings, you will have to follow a training program with an ATO (Approved Training Organisation). The list of certified ATOs is available here: <a href="https://prams.vatsim.net/atos" target="_blank">https://prams.vatsim.net/atos</a>

# Charts and procedures

## Chartfox

<a href="https://chartfox.org" target="_blank">Chartfox</a> is a free platform for VATSIM members.
It groups all chartts availble in a country's AIP with an easy presentation and bookmarking capability. These charts are a must to navigate the complex grounds and crowded upper airspaces.

## The SIA (Service d'Information Aéronautique)
The <a href="https://www.sia.aviation-civile.gouv.fr" target="_blank">SIA</a> is the official source of aeronautical information in France.
On the SIA's website you can find:
- The AIP (Aeronautical Information Publication) containing all aeronautical information for France, from restricted areas to runways physical characteristics,
- The VAC Atlas, a groupment of visual approch charts for your VFR flights,
- DGAC's phraséology manual,
- And much more !
