# LFRS - Nantes Atlantique

> This document is intent for flight simulation on VATSIM exclusively. It must not be used outside of this scope.
{.is-warning}

Nantes is an international airport located to the South-East of Nantes.
The airport has a single runway oriented North/South with an ILS to RWY03 and an offset non-precision approach to RWY21.

Transition altitude is 5 000ft and all flight regimes are allowed.
