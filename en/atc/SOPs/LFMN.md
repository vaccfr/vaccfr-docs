# LFMN - Nice Côte d'Azur
> This document is intent for flight simulation on VATSIM exclusively. It must not be used outside of this scope.
{.is-warning}

Nice Côte d'Azur (LFMN) is an international airport located approximately 6 kilometers southwest of downtown Nice, in the Alpes-Maritimes department. It is the third-busiest French airport in terms of passenger traffic, behind Paris-Charles-de-Gaulle and Paris-Orly, with more than 15 million passengers in 2025.

The airport has three terminals: Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 for commercial aviation, and a dedicated terminal for private aviation. The airport has two parallel runways, respectively handling arrivals (04L/22R) and departures (04R/22L).

The transition altitude is 5,000 ft, and both IFR and VFR flights are permitted.

> On VATSIM, a Tier 1 rating is required to open a position at LFMN. Please request access to the corresponding course from the ATC Training Department on <a href="https://learn.vatsim.fr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">learn.vatsim.fr</a>.
{.is-info}

> **LFMN** is a **CDM airport on VATSIM**. We strongly recommend reading [this document](/en/atc/documentation/CDM)  before proceeding.
{.is-info}
