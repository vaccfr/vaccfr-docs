# Approach position
## Sector
For sectorisation and positions grouping, refer to the [diagram](/en/atc/SOPs/LFRS/diagrams) section of this SOP.

## Departures

All departures climb initially to 5000ft, further climb is given to remain below arriving traffic. The ANG SID has 2 variants with an early western turn instead of the standard eastern turn; they can be issued in coordination between APP and TWR/GND in order to avoid creating a conflict with an arriving traffic East of the field.

## Arrivals
Initial approaches only exist for the same navigation reference of the approach they lead to, this means the ILS or VOR approaches only have conventional initial approaches.

To mitigate this, arrivals coming from downwind to the ILS 03 are given vectors instead of the conventional initial approach. Straight-in, arrivals are sequenced with vectors then given a Direct routing to the **I**nitial **F**ix (IF) in accordance with the 45 degress interception rule, or a heading to the Localizer.
Traffic for RWY 21 coming from the South are usually issued a direct to one of the RS398/400/402/404 fixes, or to IRS21 after a longer downwind if sequencing requires it.

### Rates
Due to single runway operations combined with the lack of high speed exits, the arrival/departure rate is relatively low: approximately 44 movements, of which 20 arrivals, ie. about <u>one arrival every 3 minutes</u>.
If there is **no departure** between two arrivals, spacing can be reduced to two and a half minutes.

## Missed Approaches

On runway 21, there are no published means of rejoining the published missed approach track if the missed approach is initiated after the MAPT. A recent (July 2026) NOTAM has been issued to mitigate this problem, but simulator pilots might not all be aware of this.

Traffic on standard missed approach climbing to 4'000ft can interfere with the standard VFR traffic pattern, as well as with the preceding departure in some cases. A lower altitude (2' or 3'000ft) can be issued to help resolve the conflict. No vectors should be issued below 2'000ft (MVA).

# Satellite airports
## LFRN - Rennes Saint-Jacques
Arrivals transit the TMA and should be delivered at FL120.
Southbound departures from Rennes are delivered at FL110.

## LFRS - Saint-Nazaire
Arrivals are vectored to **IRZ25** for the RNP 25 (with 90 degress or less if used as IAF) or MT NDB for the NDB 25.
Transfers to tower shall be made at the latest **11NM from touchdown**.

## Other airfields
There are a few other airfields within the lateral boundaries of LFRS airspace without any ATC facilities but with IFR procedures (eg. LFEY). Standard practice for traffic in or out of such airfields is to provide ATC services while inside Nantes's controlled airspace, and to release them for departure/arrival in class G.
