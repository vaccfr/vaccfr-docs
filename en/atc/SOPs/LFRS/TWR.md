# Tower position
Nantes CTR is a class D airspace spanning from surface to 1'500ft AMSL. Just above is Nantes TMA 1, also a class D airspace managed by tower from 1'500 to 2'500ft AMSL.

## Runway
Nantes has a single runway 03/21.

QFU 03 is preferential up to 5kts of tailwind component for both noise abatement and IFR procedure minima. This is because runway 03 has a straight-in precision approach (CAT III ILS) while runway 21 has 2 offset (12°) non-precision approaches (a VOR and an RNP approach) as well as a new RNP (AR) ending as a straight-in.

> Nantes is know for its very specific runway curvature: aircraft at one end of the runway cannot see the opposite threshold.
{.is-info}


### Rates
Due to single runway operations combined with the lack of high speed exits, the arrival/departure rate is relatively low, approximately 44 mouvements/h of which 20 arrivals, ie. about one arrival every 3 minutes.
If there is no departure between two arrivals, separation can be reduced to 2,5 minutes.

### Holding points
Departures usually use the entire runway length for departure (holding point F or A).
In all cases priority shall be given to IFR trafic regardless of VFR wait times.

From runway 03:
- VFR trafic depart from B unless they request otherwise,
- IFR always departs from A. B is available on request for approach category B aircaft or below (eg. ATR, TBM, small business jets …)

From runway 21:
- F is standard unless E is requested and operationally feasable.

Arriving traffic on runway 03 should when able vacate via C, D or E to avoid the LOC critical area. The runway exit to be used shall be coordinated with the GND controler (when present) to safeguard any conflicting movement.

When arriving on runway 21, traffic is expected to vacate via B.

### Clearance restriction
As a working method, <u>no take-off clearances shall be issued</u> when an arriving traffic is at, or less than, 3NM from the threshold.

## VFR
### Entries and exits
VFR traffic shall enter the CTR at 1500ft and exit at 1000ft (both AMSL). Traffic leaving LFRS can be cleared up to 2500ft without coordination. Higher altitudes shall be requested by the pilot or coordinated with the appropriate APP or FIS.

> When exiting the CTR/TMA1: "Flight Information available on 1xx.xxx"

Entries and exits shall be made via the **V**isual **R**eporting **P**oints (VRPs) at the altitudes mentionned above unless coordinated otherwise.

> Overflying the city of Nantes is prohibited below 5000ft, except for MEDEVAC helicopters and IFR flights following a procedure.
{.is-warning}

### Traffic pattern

Traffic pattern are done to the North-West of the airfield, hence right-hand patterns on runway 21.
Pattern altitude is **1100**ft AMSL (1000ft AGL).
![lfrs-pattern.png](/doc-atc/lfrr/lfrs/lfrs-pattern.png =50%x){.align-right}
It is possible to clear traffic on an South-Eastern pattern, this is especially useful to prevent air proximity events in case of a missed approach on runway 21.

Both patterns can be used to hold 2 VFR traffic each, one at each end of the downwind leg.

There is a specific helicopter pattern for each runway.

### Helicopters
Rotorcraft are handled as general VFR traffic.
Special attention shall be given to MEDEVAC & SAMU missions. Both hospital helipads are located within the CTR and one of them is about 4NM under the approach path of runway 21.

In the event of an urgent[^1] departure from "CHU de Nantes" and an arrival on runway 21, it is possible to clear the helicopter with a mandatory two-way traffic information. Depending on the urgency of the medical evacuation, priority shall be given to the medical situation and the arriving IFR traffic should perform a missed approach.

[^1]: VATSIM POL SO reminder : Pilot VSOA approval or ATC coordination and acceptance required beforehand.
