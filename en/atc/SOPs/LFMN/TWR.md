# Tower Position

The Nice CTR is a **class D airspace** extending from the **surface up to 3,500 feet**. It borders the Mandelieu CTR to the west. The other airspaces adjacent to the Nice CTR are managed by Nice Approach.

## Runways
### General information

> Runway **04L/22R** is used for **arrivals**.
{.is-info}


Runway 04L/22R can also be used for VFR departures or Light aircraft.

> Runway **04R/22L** is used for **departures**.
{.is-info}

Indeed, the outer runway is used for departures, contrary to common practice. This is one of Nice's distinctive features, and is due to noise abatement constraints.

These **two runways** form a closely-spaced, specialized runway pair and are therefore **interdependent**. This means **it is not possible to have two aircraft taking off or landing at the same time**.

### Limits

The **QFU 043 is preferential**, given the approach minima, weather conditions and the terrain's topography.

> QFU 043 is used up to a tailwind component of 6 knots (inclusive).
{.is-info}

## Rate

In normal operation (runways used as described above), the rate is 38 to 45 movements per hour.

During single-runway operations, the rate is reduced to between 30 and 37 movements per hour.

## Runway vacating and crossing

Due to its design and the specialization of the runway pair, only departing traffic needs to cross the inner runway. **Crossing clearances are the responsibility of the Tower controller**, including taxi operations between the two runways.

> **Conditional crossing clearances may not be given**. A crossing clearance may be issued as soon as the traffic occupying the runway passes, or has passed, the holding point from which the crossing will take place.
{.is-danger}

## Assigning holding points

- Medium traffic taxis to holding point Q3
- Heavy traffic taxis to holding point W3

> By default, the Ground controller will offer Light traffic (business jets in particular) a departure from B3. The TORA is 2157m. Remember to coordinate a departure from holding point Q3 if traffic load becomes incompatible with the use of B3.
{.is-warning}


> **A3 intersection is not usable** and is closed due to its immediate proximity after crossing runway 04L.
{.is-danger}

## Arriving traffic
When vacating the runway, and to avoid blocking the runway or the holding points due to the proximity of taxiway U, an initial taxi route after crossing can be coordinated with the GND controller.

Here is an example for traffic arriving on 04L:

> **AFR22RJ, taxi left on U, contact ground 121.705, goodbye.**

On QFU 043, the inner runway has only one rapid exit taxiway: EG.

## Go-around
On VATSIM, at Nice, in the event of a go-around, the following are systematically issued:
- a heading
- an altitude

> These two parameters must be coordinated beforehand with the Approach controller if present (LFMN_F_APP or LFMN_APP if the former is absent).
{.is-warning}


> In the case of Nice, and to avoid any confusion, **the phrase "go around as published" must not be used**.
{.is-danger}


The MAP[1] to be followed differs depending on whether the traffic is still flying an instrument approach or is established on a VPT.

> Inform the Approach controller of the go-around and update the tag accordingly, wherever possible.
{.is-info}

## VFR management
![vac-lfmn.png](/doc-atc/vac-lfmn.png)

### Traffic pattern

The traffic pattern is at 1,000 feet to the south of the airport, regardless of the runway configuration in use.

> Due to the proximity of the traffic pattern to IFR departure and arrival tracks, **we suggest not to accept traffic in the pattern when arrival load is high**. In this case, it is preferable to offer Nice departures a local flight or a navigation exercise instead.
{.is-info}

### General Aviation Traffic

The Nice CTR has 4 entry points (EW, NA, SB and EC) and 2 named VFR transit routes. These are located to the North (N1) and North-West (W1) of the airport, running respectively from NA and WE towards WN (1,500ft minimum).

Integrations from the North (via WN) are cleared at 1,500ft overhead the airport, to join the traffic pattern to the south of the airport at 1,000ft.

Arrivals from the South and North-East, as well as coastal transits, are conducted between 500ft and 1,000ft maximum, in order to pass below IFR arrivals before joining the traffic pattern.

For all VFR traffic, speed is limited to 160 kts throughout the CTR. Any operation at a higher speed must be the subject of a specific clearance request.

> Special VFR is not authorized.
{.is-danger}

### Helicopters

For rotary-wing traffic, the transit altitude is limited to 500ft. They must be cleared to arrival points (HS or HE) for the various FATOs.

The FATOs are used in the same direction as the runways (e.g. 04L/R = MS for landing and ME for takeoff).

For helicopters departing from or arriving at Monaco, they must be cleared to EC and then given traffic information if necessary, as the **Monaco CTR is not covered by Nice Tower**.
