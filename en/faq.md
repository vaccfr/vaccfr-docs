## What is French vACC?
We are the official French community of virtual pilots and controllers on the VATSIM network. The French vACC is responsible for **air traffic control** _over mainland France_ as well as for the training of controllers.

In addition, we also train new pilots in the phraseology, procedures and particularities of flying on the network.

## How to join French vACC?

Like any VATSIM member, you can join any vACC.

You can apply to become an affiliated member of the French vACC by going to : https://core.vateud.net/ and selecting "French vACC" as your **"current vACC"**.

This is a mandatory step if you are planning to start ATC training in France.

## How to become a virtual Air Traffic Controller?

You are an aviation enthusiast, your dream is to become an air traffic controller, or you want to move on to the other side of the radio after many hours of flying on VATSIM ... It's all good! You are in the right place to start _your career as a virtual air traffic controller on VATSIM_.

First, apply to become an affiliated French vACC is described in above questions.

Then go to https://learn.vatsim.fr/ . You will have access to a training module called **Ab-initio**.

The first part of the course is self-paced, with a _theory basics_ to validate. Once you've completed the theory, you can _request to be put on the waiting list_ to request a session with a mentor.

The training waiting list operates on a first come, first served basis. French vACC Training Department contacts students at regular intervals to open training phases.

⚠️ While waiting to be contacted, we strongly recommend that **candidates carry out observation sessions on the network (OBS)**.

Please note that this is a long-term training course given by volunteer mentors. Moreover, **investment from your side is expected throughout the mentoring** to ensure a smooth progress.

## How to become a visitting controller ?

You are an air traffic controller on VATSIM and a member of another vACC but you wish to provide ATC service in France occasionally?

This is possible if you hold at least an S3 rating. Before submitting your request, please make sure you meet the prerequisites explained here: https://vatsim.net/docs/policy/transfer-and-visiting-controller-policy

If so, we look forward to receive your application here: https://core.vateud.net/my/visit . Formulate your motivation and your wishes for airports in France for which you'd like to provide ATC service. Please note that Tier1 and Tier2 platforms are also available to visitors, but only at a later stage.

Once your application has been approved, you will be given access to a Visiting training module at https://learn.vatsim.fr/. This module is given in English and gives you everything you need to control the network in France. Of course, speaking French is not compulsory, but it is an asset.

⚠️ **The waiting list is long. Please be patient.**

## Are there qualification equivalences if I am an ATCO in real life or if I provide ATC service on IVAO?

The answer is yes! A "fast-track" procedure is available for real-life air traffic controllers. This procedure is also available to IVAO controllers holding APC or ACC ratings. This procedure entitles the holder to a S2 rating (Tower) or S3 (Approach) respectively.

Equivalence is neither automatic nor instantaneous. This procedure is not designed to train controllers: it focuses on a brief introduction to the tools and on a check of the skills acquired.

If you would like to perform a fast-track or require further information, please go to https://support.vatsim.fr/

## How to contact French vACC staff?

## What are the pilot ranks and how do I obtain them?

On VATSIM, pilot ranks are established as follows, chronologically:

- **P0** beginner pilot,
- **P1** private pilot (Private Pilot License),
- **P2** Instrument Rating,
- **P3** Commercial Pilot License / Multi-Engine Piston,
- **P4** Airline Transport Pilot License,

If you are an IRL pilot, you can apply to VATSIM for the rating corresponding to your profile.

If you wish to obtain these ratings, you must undergo training with an ATO **(Approved Training Organisation)**. The list of certified ATOs is available at this link : https://prams.vatsim.net/atos

## How are VATSIM entities structured?

VATSIM network is structured into diffent entities, more or less dependent on each other and each providing various services.

First of all, there are the **vACCs**, then the **divisions** such as VATEUD (grouping of several vACCs) and then the **regions** such as VATEMEA (grouping of several divisions).

Above all of this, there is the **VATSIM Board of Governors (BoG)**, responsible for all the network.

## What are the ATC ranks and what do they stand for?

Air traffic controller ratings are distributed as follows, in chronological order of training:

- **OBS** : Observer,
- **S1** : Beginner Student (Ground / Tower),
- **S2** : Tower Controller / Approach Student,
- **S3** : Approach Controller / Enroute Student,
- **C1** : Enroute Controller,
- **C3** : Senior Enroute Controller,

The other controller ratings are "administrative ratings".

## What are the ATC tools used by French vACC?

Our controllers use Euroscope to provide an air traffic control service on VATSIM.

French vACC has also developed Cofrance, a plugin that can be installed in addition to Euroscope.

To find out how to install these tools and connect to the network as an observer, please consult the dedicated sheet in the documentation: https://vatsim.fr/app/documentation
