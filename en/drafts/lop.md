# Local Operating Procedures
This documentation defines the coordination procedures applicable between Approach Control Units (ACUs), Area Control Centres (ACCs), and Upper Area Control (UAC) sectors within the French FIRs for the provision of Air Traffic Services (ATS) to IFR and VFR traffic on the VATSIM network.

> The procedures and information contained herein are intended exclusively for use within the VATSIM simulation environment and shall not be used for real-world operations.
> {.is-warning}
