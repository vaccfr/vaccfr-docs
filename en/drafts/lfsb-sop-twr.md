# Tower Position
The **Bâle-Mulhouse CTR (LFSB)** is **Class D airspace**, extending from the **surface up to 2,000 ft**.

The CTR surrounds the Bâle-Mulhouse airport and provides controlled airspace for aircraft operating in the vicinity of the aerodrome. 

![lfsb_ctr.png](/doc-atc/lfee/lfsb/lfsb_ctr.png)

## Runways
Bale-Mulhouse has two sets of runways 07/25 and 15/33. Runways 07/25

RWY 07
* Take-offs are permitted **only during daytime VFR operations** and for **home-based aircraft**.
* **Landing is not permitted**.

RWY 25
* Circling is **prohibited south of the runway**.
* **Right-hand circuit**.
