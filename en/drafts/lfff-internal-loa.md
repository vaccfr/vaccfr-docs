# General

The purpose of this Letter of Agreement is to define the coordination procedures to be applied between **Paris ACC** and Approach control units within Paris FIR when providing ATS to IFR traffic.

These approach control units will be referred to as:
- De Gaulle APP (LFPG)
- Orly APP (LFPO)
- Beauvais APP (LFOB)
- Lille APP (LFQQ)
- Seine APP (LFPM)


> **Caution!** This document is intended for training purposes on the VATSIM network, and is therefore strictly for simulation use only.
{.is-danger}

# Procedures for Coordination

### Silent Handoff

Flights may be handed over without any prior coordination, if all the following conditions are met:
- Route via a valid coordination point (COP)
- Appropriate level for the relevant route

### Release

Unless otherwise specified the transfer of control occurs with the transfer of communication.
